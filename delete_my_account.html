Delete your Tell Da account

Last updated: 26 May 2026

You can request deletion of your Tell Da account and all associated data without installing the app. Email us using the steps below.

Deleting your account removes:

  • Your Google account link — the Google subject identifier and email we use to sign you in.
  • Your summaries — every YouTube summary you've generated, along with the original URLs and titles.
  • Your notes — anything you typed into the notes field on a summary.
  • Your wallet balance — any remaining paise in your in-app wallet.
  • Your purchase history — except for billing records we are legally required to retain (see Timeline below).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to soodanish.06@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Account deletion request.
  3. Send it from the email address associated with your Google sign-in — this is how we verify the request is from you.
  4. You don't need to include any other information. We'll match your email to your account and process the deletion.

Deletion completes within 30 days of receiving your request.

Timeline

  • Within 7 days: we'll reply confirming we received your request and have started processing it.
  • Within 30 days: your account record, summaries, notes, and wallet balance are fully removed from our servers.
  • Billing records: Google Play purchase tokens and the associated transaction records may be retained for the minimum period required by Indian tax and consumer protection law (typically up to 7 years). These records contain only the purchase amount, date, and SKU — no personal content.

If you're not sure whether you have an account

If you've ever opened the Tell Da app and tapped Sign in with Google, you have an account on our servers. Even if you never generated a summary, a sign-in record exists. Email us using the steps above and we'll check.
